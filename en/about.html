I'm Frank van der Vaart, a freelance IT architect based in the Rotterdam-The Hague region. Through VSDA Solutions & Consultancy, I help organizations align their IT architecture with their business strategy. Pragmatic, human, and with a sharp eye for what actually works in practice.

My career started in 1985 as an HP3000 Systems Engineer at Shell Pernis Refinery. What followed was a career spanning more than 35 years, growing from hands-on engineer to Lead Architect at one of the largest companies in the world.

In the spring of 2025, I made a deliberate pause. For nearly a year, I traveled through Europe in my camper van, time to reflect and recharge. The trigger wasn't just fatigue, but also a growing sense of frustration: the energy transition at Shell was moving too slowly for me, and was even being rolled back in places. That experience reinforced my conviction that sustainability isn't an afterthought.

"Not just delivering a solution, but making sure people can do their work easier and faster afterwards. That's my measure of a job well done."

Since April 2026, I've been active as an independent IT specialist. I choose assignments based on content and complexity, but sustainability plays a role in that choice too.

I'm also interested in cybersecurity, and enjoy raising awareness around it. Simple measures make the biggest difference. I still regularly meet people who don't use a password manager and have been reusing the same passwords across multiple websites for years.
